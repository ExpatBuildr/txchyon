## 📌 Introduction — Why Banking Matters for Crypto Businesses

Crypto businesses often hit a wall with traditional banks. Many banks are hesitant to work with crypto projects due to regulations, volatility, or AML/KYC concerns.  

Without proper banking, it’s hard to:  

- Pay employees in fiat or stablecoins  
- Manage revenue and expenses  
- Connect to exchanges  
- Maintain credibility with investors  

This guide walks you through **crypto-friendly banking solutions**, stablecoin integrations, and international options so your business can run smoothly.

---

## 🏦 Section 1: Traditional Banks That Work With Crypto

Some banks have embraced crypto companies, especially in the US and Europe:  

- **Silvergate Bank (US)** – Known for crypto business accounts and real-time USD/crypto transfers  
- **Signature Bank (US)** – Offers blockchain-based payment solutions  
- **Custodia (EU)** – Focused on crypto custody and banking for startups  

> Tip: Always check your bank’s **KYC/AML policies** before opening an account.

---

## 💰 Section 2: Stablecoin and Digital Payment Solutions

If traditional banks aren’t an option, stablecoins can fill the gap:  

- **USDC and USDT** – Widely accepted for payroll, vendor payments, and transfers  
- **Circle Business Accounts** – Supports USDC management for companies  
- **Multi-sig wallets** – Secure treasury management for crypto funds  

Stablecoins reduce friction and **keep your business liquid**, especially for international teams.

---

## 🌍 Section 3: International Crypto Banking

Crypto businesses often operate globally. Consider these strategies:  

- **Offshore crypto-friendly banks** – Some Caribbean and European banks accept crypto businesses  
- **Fintech solutions** – Revolut, Wise, Payoneer (where regulations allow)  
- **Jurisdiction selection** – Incorporating in a crypto-friendly country can simplify banking

## 🛠 Section 4: Integrating Banking With Your Business Workflow

- Connect your business entity to **bank accounts** (see [Crypto Entity Formation](https://txchyon.com/blog/regulatory-tax/crypto-entity-formation))  
- Use accounting software to track **revenue, expenses, and crypto movements**  
- Integrate with **tax reporting tools** ([Crypto Tax Reporting](https://txchyon.com/blog/regulatory-tax/crypto-tax-reporting-software))  

> Pro Tip: Keep personal and business banking **strictly separate** to simplify taxes and audits.

---

## 📌 Section 5: Key Considerations Before Choosing a Bank

1. **Regulatory Compliance** – Is the bank compliant with AML/KYC laws?  
2. **Crypto Exposure** – Does the bank allow crypto deposits or only fiat?  
3. **Transaction Speed & Fees** – Consider transfers between wallets and exchanges  
4. **International Accessibility** – Especially important for global teams  

---

## 📌 Conclusion — Keep Your Crypto Business Liquid

Banking isn’t glamorous, but it’s the backbone of your crypto business. Choose the right bank, integrate with stablecoins and accounting tools, and **you’ll avoid headaches later**.  

Remember: a solid banking setup keeps your business operational, compliant, and ready to scale.
