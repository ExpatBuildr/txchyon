## What Is Crypto Tax-Loss Harvesting?

Crypto tax-loss harvesting is the practice of **selling underperforming assets at a loss** to offset capital gains from other trades or investments.

Key benefits:
- Reduce taxable income  
- Free up capital for reinvestment  
- Improve long-term portfolio efficiency  

If you’re not sure how your allocation affects this, review:  
👉 **[Crypto Portfolio Allocation for Beginners](https://txchyon.com/blog/portfolio-management/crypto-portfolio-allocation-for-beginners/)**

---

## Why Tax-Loss Harvesting Matters

Crypto is volatile. Market dips can create opportunities to:
- offset gains from profitable trades  
- reduce overall tax liability  
- reinvest without extra capital  

It’s a **legal and strategic tool**, not a loophole.

---

## When to Harvest Losses

### 1️⃣ Year-End Harvesting
- Review underperforming positions before closing your books  
- Best for simple portfolios

### 2️⃣ Opportunistic Harvesting
- Sell during dips throughout the year  
- Reinvest immediately into similar assets  
- Avoid emotional decisions

---

## Step-by-Step: How to Perform Crypto Tax-Loss Harvesting

1. **Identify Losses**
   - Compare current price vs cost basis  
   - Track realized and unrealized losses

2. **Check Allocation & Exposure**
   - Don’t let harvesting break your portfolio balance  
   - Use insights from:  
     **[How to Rebalance a Crypto Portfolio](https://txchyon.com/blog/portfolio-management/how-to-rebalance-a-crypto-portfolio/)**

3. **Sell Underperforming Assets**
   - Trigger losses legally

4. **Offset Gains**
   - Apply losses against realized gains for tax purposes

5. **Reinvest if Needed**
   - Maintain target allocation  
   - Avoid violating wash sale rules (if applicable)

---

## Common Mistakes in Tax-Loss Harvesting

❌ Selling purely for tax reasons without considering portfolio balance  
❌ Rebuying the same asset immediately (wash sale issues in some jurisdictions)  
❌ Ignoring transaction fees and slippage  
❌ Failing to document trades properly  
❌ Harvesting too aggressively in volatile markets  

Correct execution keeps your **strategy intact while reducing taxes**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I harvest losses multiple times per year?
Yes, opportunistic harvesting is common.

### Will harvesting affect my portfolio performance?
Properly executed, it maintains target allocation and risk.

### Is this legal in all countries?
Check local regulations. In the US, wash sale rules for crypto are still evolving.
